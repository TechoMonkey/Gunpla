# Starter equipment catalog

Prototype draft v0.1 · November 20, 2026

Equip components to add orders, counterattacks, passive stat modifiers, and capabilities to a chassis. Component points and the combat entries below are initial playtest proposals. The approved chassis values, Move and Cool orders, and foundation rules govern the assembled suit.

Every attack order lists its own attack dice, damage per hit, and SP. Attach the component's listed range and hit profile to each of its attack orders when displaying the assembled order card. Apply relevant upgrade modifiers before showing those final values.

Every granted order identifies its source component. Choose one available normal order per activation. Equipment selection happens while building the team and is revealed before deployment.

The two starter chassis each have 2 weapon slots, 1 shield slot, and 3 upgrade slots. Every item below uses one slot of its listed category. General and specialized upgrades share the upgrade-slot capacity. Specialized upgrades also require the chassis compatibility printed on the card.

Weapons and upgrades are unique per Gunpla by default. A card must explicitly allow additional copies to override that limit; follow any maximum it states. Each allowed copy consumes its listed slots and points. Different suits may each equip their own copy of an item. All weapons and upgrades in this initial catalog use the default one-copy limit.

### Heat Hawk Counter

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | Interrupt |
| Weight | 15 |
| Attack dice | 3d6 |
| Hit requirement | 4+ |
| Damage per hit | 2 |
| SP | 0 |
| Reach | 2 inches |
| Heat cost | +1 heat |
| Uses | Once per round by that suit |

It triggers after an enemy declares a ranged attack from a normal order and chooses its target, before attack dice or interception rolls. The ranged attack must still be pending. The shooter must be within 2 inches and in torso-to-torso line of sight. The enemy's selected target can be the equipped suit or another legal target.

Attack the triggering shooter with the statistics above. Resolve the counterattack's heat check and consequences. A defeated shooter loses its remaining normal order. A surviving shooter resumes its pending action if it is still legal.

### Shield Bash

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | Interrupt |
| Weight | 10 |
| Heat cost | +1 heat |
| Uses | Once per round by that suit |
| Effect | Cancel one pending ranged attack step against another operational allied Gunpla. |

It triggers after an enemy declares a ranged attack from a normal order against another operational allied Gunpla, before attack dice or interception rolls. The ranged attack must still be pending. The shooter must be within 2 inches of the equipped suit and in torso-to-torso line of sight.

Cancel that one ranged attack step. The step is spent. Resolve the counterattack's heat check and consequences, then finish any remaining legal steps in the enemy's normal order and apply that order's normal end-of-activation heat. The cancellation takes effect when Shield Bash resolves.

## Flight Backpack

This specialized Flight upgrade costs 25 points and uses one upgrade slot. It requires a chassis explicitly marked as supporting Flight upgrades. It grants the passive capability to use Flight and the protected Flight toggle order. Among the two HG starter chassis, the GM meets this requirement and the Zaku II is incompatible.

| Order | Phase | Weight | Effect |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Flight | 1 | 10 | While grounded, enable Flight and enter Hover. |

Use the foundation's flight bands, movement costs, attack-range bonus, high-altitude Shielding bonus, targeting restrictions, landing, and crash rules. Movement changes altitude after Flight has been enabled. Landing through movement switches Flight off.

Landing is available when the chassis permits it. Follow the chassis's movement restrictions when determining legal end states.

The Flight capability and its toggle order are protected from heat-based disabling.

## Reinforced Plating

This General upgrade costs 20 points, uses one upgrade slot, and grants a passive +1 Armor modifier. It is compatible with either starter chassis when upgrade-slot capacity permits. Apply the modifier once while the upgrade is equipped. The passive modifier is protected from heat-based disabling.

## Availability and upgrades

The chassis supplies Move, Cool, its base stats, and its native passive capabilities. Equipment grants the additional entries described on its component card. Apply listed passive modifiers once when calculating assembled stats, then apply temporary bonuses when orders resolve during play.

For a movement step, calculate the order's allowance from its printed rule and the suit's current Movement stat, then apply any active Flight-band movement bonus once to that step. For Advance and Fire, calculate half Movement first, then add that band's bonus.

Heat-based disabling is tracked per normal order or counterattack. Other granted entries retain their own availability, and equipped passive modifiers continue to apply. Cooling restores heat-disabled entries while preserving already-spent use limits. The core heat protections for Cool, passives, Jump, and Flight continue to apply.

Equip items only when their slot costs fit the chassis's capacity and all printed compatibility requirements are met. Chassis movement restrictions govern the legal states and actions of the assembled build. An item changes a chassis restriction only when its text explicitly states that change.

The four starter builds each equip two distinct weapons and one shield. Zaku A carries Machine Gun, Zaku B carries Bazooka, GM A carries Beam Spray Gun, and GM B carries Beam Rifle. Each Zaku also carries Heat Hawk and Shoulder Shield; each GM also carries Beam Saber and GM Shield.
